The problem

AI can write. That's not the hard part.

The hard part is getting it to sound like you, stop inventing square footage, and stay away from phrases like "perfect for young families" that can get you in trouble. Random prompt lists do not fix that. A system does.

This guide gives you that system: a one-time Agent Profile that teaches the AI your voice, a "fact lock" step that stops it from guessing, and a Fair Housing scan at the end of every workflow.

Typical AI draft"Welcome to your dream home! Perfect for growing families, this stunning gem sits in a safe neighborhood close to top-rated schools."
After the workflow"Sunlight fills the updated kitchen, with quartz counters and a walk-in pantry added in 2023. Three bedrooms upstairs, a fenced yard, and a 0.4-mile walk to Glenmont Park."

The rewrite describes features and verifiable distances, not people, safety or school quality.

Fill in the brackets, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat tool, and go. No coding, no plugins, no new software to learn.

Prompt 2 · Fact LockFor this entire conversation, you may only state facts that appear in this material. If something would be useful but is missing, write [CONFIRM: what is needed] instead of guessing. Do not invent measurements, brands, school names, distances or features. Reply only with "Facts locked" and a numbered list of any obvious gaps I should fill before we write.

Any general AI chat tool works, including the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Paid versions give you longer conversations and saved instructions, but they are not required.

Generally yes, but you remain responsible for everything you publish. The guide builds in fact checks and a Fair Housing review for exactly that reason. Always follow your brokerage policy and MLS rules.

I'm not technical. Will I be able to use this?

Yes. If you can copy and paste text, you can use every workflow. There is no software to install.
