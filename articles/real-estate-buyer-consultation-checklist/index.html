Real estate buyer consultation checklist: questions, agenda, and AI prompts

By Plain English Library · Published October 3, 2026

A real estate buyer consultation checklist helps you leave the first meeting with a clear search brief: what the buyer needs, what remains uncertain, and who will do what next. Use the checklist below with a suggested 30-minute agenda, 12 buyer consultation questions, and an AI prompt for organizing your preparation.

This template is for agents preparing a conversation. It does not replace your brokerage's forms, a lender's assessment, or legal advice. Adapt the timing to the buyer; an agreement discussion or a complex move may need a separate appointment.

The buyer consultation checklist

Before the meeting

  • Confirm the time, location or video link, and who the buyer wants to include in the conversation.
  • Ask whether the buyer already has a representation agreement and follow your broker's procedure before proceeding.
  • Send a short agenda and explain which topics you plan to cover.
  • Gather your approved explanation of services, compensation, and any required agreement or disclosures.
  • Prepare current, sourced market information if you plan to discuss it. Note the date and area it covers.
  • Ask the buyer to think about timing, essential property features, and questions. Direct financial documents through the appropriate secure process rather than a casual message thread.

During the meeting

  • Hear the buyer's reason for moving and distinguish a target date from a firm deadline.
  • Separate essential property features from preferences and identify trade-offs the buyer would consider.
  • Clarify the price range the buyer wants you to search, the status of financing discussions, and questions for the lender.
  • Explain your services and the applicable agreement using your broker-approved materials.
  • Agree on how you will share properties, collect showing feedback, and communicate about time-sensitive matters.
  • Record open questions with a named person responsible for answering them.

After the meeting

  • Send a short summary and ask the buyer to correct anything you misunderstood.
  • Record the agreed search criteria in your approved client system.
  • Assign each next step an owner and a date, including your own tasks.
  • Complete the applicable agreement and disclosure steps before arranging activities that require them.

Keep the checklist short enough to use while listening. A completed form helps only if the buyer recognizes their priorities in your summary.

12 real estate buyer consultation questions

Use these as a conversation guide. You do not need to read them in order or ask for sensitive personal details that do not help define the property search.

  1. What do you want this move to make easier? Listen for a practical need such as less maintenance, a workspace, or a shorter trip to a destination the buyer names.
  2. Which date matters most, and how much flexibility do you have? Ask what would happen if the search took longer.
  3. Have you bought a home before, and which part would you like explained? Let the buyer set the level of detail.
  4. What price range do you want me to use? Confirm the buyer's preferred search limit rather than treating the lender's maximum as a spending target.
  5. Where are you in your conversation with a lender? Record the stage and questions to take back, not an AI assessment of eligibility.
  6. Which three property features would be hardest to compromise on? Ask what each feature needs to do for the buyer.
  7. Which preferences could change for the right property? Use the answer to discuss layouts and property choices.
  8. Which locations or destinations should guide the search? Use the buyer's stated criteria and verifiable property information.
  9. How do you feel about repairs or ongoing maintenance? Clarify the types of work they would consider; leave costs and condition assessments to appropriate professionals.
  10. What has worried or confused you so far? Write down questions you cannot answer on the spot.
  11. How should we communicate, and when are you available? Agree on an approach for routine updates and urgent decisions.
  12. What would make our next conversation useful? End with a specific task or decision to prepare for.

For example, “I need a home office” may mean a separate room, space for a desk, or a place for confidential calls. Ask which requirement matters. Do not decide on behalf of the buyer that a particular property or neighborhood suits “people like them.”

Do not rush a buyer through an agreement to keep the meeting within 30 minutes. Send approved materials ahead when appropriate and allow time for questions.

Discuss representation and financing with the right materials

NAR's Written Buyer Agreements 101 says an MLS participant working with a buyer must enter into a written agreement before an in-person or live virtual home tour under its policy. Check the rules applicable to your practice and state with your broker; do not present this checklist as a universal statement of law.

Explain the services, duration, compensation, and relevant termination terms in the actual agreement. NAR's consumer guide to negotiating agreements explains that compensation is negotiable and not set by law. Avoid promising that a seller will cover a buyer's obligation. Use the approved agreement to explain the options and responsibilities.

On financing, the CFPB explains that a preapproval is conditional, not a guaranteed loan offer. Ask the buyer which questions remain for their lender. Keep the amount they feel comfortable spending separate from the amount a lender might approve.

An AI prompt to prepare the buyer consultation

AI can organize an agenda from redacted notes. Use your brokerage's approved tools and remove names, email addresses, account numbers, financial documents, and details that could identify the buyer. Our client privacy checklist for AI tools covers this preparation.

Help me prepare a real estate buyer consultation.
Use only these redacted notes: [paste non-identifying notes].
Meeting length: [minutes].
My approved meeting topics: [paste topics, not confidential documents].

Return:
1. A short agenda with suggested time allocations.
2. Five questions to clarify missing or conflicting information.
3. A search brief with separate sections for essential features, preferences, and unknowns.
4. A next-step table with task, proposed owner, and date to confirm.

Do not infer income, creditworthiness, protected characteristics, or neighborhood suitability.
Do not invent a budget, deadline, property fact, loan approval, or agreed commitment.
Mark missing information [ASK BUYER].
Mark financing questions [ASK LENDER].
Mark agreement or legal questions [ASK BROKER].
Keep proposed tasks separate from commitments already in the notes.

A fictional example

Input: “Buyer A wants a separate workspace and low maintenance. Hopes to move in about three months. Has contacted a lender. Search budget and property type not yet confirmed.”

A useful preparation note would flag the budget and property type as unknown, ask what “low maintenance” means, and ask whether three months is flexible. It would not assign a loan amount, declare a condominium the best choice, or promise a closing date. Review the AI's brief against the original notes before using it.

For practice, ask the tool to play a buyer who has questions about your process. Give it only your approved service description. If it invents agreement terms, discard those parts and return to the actual materials.

Buyer consultation follow-up email template

Fill this in from your checked notes. Include only actions the buyer agreed to, and replace bracketed text before sending.

Subject: Your home search: priorities and next steps

Hi [First name],

Thank you for meeting with me. My notes show these priorities:
- Essential features: [confirmed features].
- Flexible preferences: [confirmed preferences].
- Search area and price range: [confirmed criteria, or what we still need to clarify].
- Timing: [target date and flexibility].

Our next steps:
- I will [agreed task] by [date].
- You plan to [agreed task] by [date].
- We still need an answer to [open question] from [person].

Our next check-in is [agreed date and method].
Please reply with anything I missed or misunderstood.

[Name and required brokerage information]

Common questions

What should be in a buyer consultation packet?

Include your agenda, a short search questionnaire, an overview of your services, approved agreement and disclosure materials, and a next-step sheet. Choose documents your buyer will use and explain where to send sensitive information.

Is a buyer questionnaire the same as a consultation?

No. A questionnaire collects starting information. During a consultation, you clarify the answers, explain your process, and agree on what happens next. A written answer such as “move soon” still needs a conversation.

Can AI write a buyer representation agreement?

Use the forms and review process your broker and legal advisers approve. The prompt in this article organizes a meeting; it is not a contract-drafting tool.

What if the buyer is not ready to tour?

Agree on a useful preparation step, such as speaking with a lender or ranking essential features. Set a follow-up date only with the buyer's agreement. You can still explain your services without assuming they are ready to make a commitment.
